Impact, Ho Chi Minh City, 2024

Seroprevalence modelling informed the Department of Health's decision to declare a measles outbreak and design the reactive vaccination campaign.

The work was recognised as one of the city's top ten medical achievements of 2024. Since then I have trained the city CDC's in-house modelling team, which now runs outbreak analyses independently.

Research Vaccination and population immunity, turning routine data into models of outbreak risk. Teaching Short courses, workshops and talks on disease modelling, statistics and R. Software Open-source R packages for modelling, biostatistics and bioinformatics. Blog A place to think out loud about disease modelling, code and the work of science.
