About

A modeller's journey

I model the spread of vaccine-preventable diseases in Vietnam, and help Ho Chi Minh City CDC build the surveillance and modelling capacity behind its outbreak response. I'm a DPhil researcher at the University of Oxford and a medical doctor by training. This is the short version, and the places the work has taken me.
